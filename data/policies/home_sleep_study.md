## Medical Necessity Criteria

Home sleep apnea testing (HSAT) is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following are met:

1. Patient has signs or symptoms suggestive of obstructive sleep apnea (snoring, witnessed apneas, excessive daytime sleepiness, or a high-risk screening score such as STOP-BANG >= 3).
2. Patient does not have significant comorbid conditions (severe COPD, neuromuscular disease, chronic opioid use, or suspected central sleep apnea) that would require in-lab polysomnography instead.
3. Patient has not had a sleep study for the same indication within the last 2 years, unless there has been a significant change in weight or symptoms.

## Documentation Requirements

- Validated screening questionnaire score (e.g., STOP-BANG, Epworth Sleepiness Scale).
- Relevant comorbidity history ruling out need for in-lab study.

## Exclusions

- HSAT is not covered for patients with suspected central sleep apnea, significant lung disease, or neuromuscular disorders -- in-lab polysomnography is required instead.
