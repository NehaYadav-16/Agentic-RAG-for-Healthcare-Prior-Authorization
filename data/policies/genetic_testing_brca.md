## Medical Necessity Criteria

BRCA1/BRCA2 genetic testing is considered medically necessary when ONE of the following applies:

1. Patient has a personal history of breast, ovarian, pancreatic, or prostate cancer with features suggestive of hereditary risk (early age of onset, triple-negative breast cancer, or bilateral disease).
2. Patient has a first- or second-degree relative with a known pathogenic BRCA1/BRCA2 mutation.
3. Patient has a family history meeting established hereditary breast/ovarian cancer risk assessment criteria (e.g., multiple affected relatives, Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry with relevant family history).
4. Patient has a personal or family history that produces a risk assessment score above the threshold on a validated hereditary cancer risk model (e.g., Tyrer-Cuzick, BRCAPRO).

## Documentation Requirements

- Detailed pedigree / family history documentation.
- Genetic counseling consultation note (pre-test counseling is required for most payers).
- Personal cancer history with pathology details if applicable.

## Exclusions

- Testing for general population screening without qualifying personal/family history is not covered.
