## Medical Necessity Criteria

Low-dose CT of the chest is considered medically necessary when ONE of the following applies:

1. Screening indication: patient is age 50-80, has a 20 pack-year or greater smoking history, and is a current smoker or has quit within the last 15 years (USPSTF lung cancer screening criteria).
2. Diagnostic indication: patient has an abnormal chest X-ray finding requiring further characterization.
3. Diagnostic indication: patient has unexplained respiratory symptoms (persistent cough, hemoptysis, unexplained weight loss) with risk factors for malignancy or infection.
4. Surveillance: patient has a known pulmonary nodule requiring follow-up per Fleischner Society guidelines.

## Documentation Requirements

- Smoking history (pack-years, current status, quit date if applicable) for screening indication.
- Prior chest X-ray report for diagnostic indication.
- Nodule size and prior CT date for surveillance indication.

## Exclusions

- Screening outside the specified age/smoking-history criteria is not covered.
