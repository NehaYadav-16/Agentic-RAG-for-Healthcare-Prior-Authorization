## Medical Necessity Criteria

A power wheelchair or power-operated vehicle is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following are met:

1. Patient has a mobility limitation that significantly impairs ability to participate in activities of daily living within the home.
2. Patient is unable to self-propel a manual wheelchair, or a manual wheelchair is insufficient due to the severity of the mobility limitation and the patient has sufficient upper extremity function and cognition to safely operate a power wheelchair.
3. A face-to-face mobility evaluation by the treating physician has been completed and documented within the last 6 months.
4. The home environment can accommodate the device (confirmed via home assessment).

## Documentation Requirements

- Face-to-face evaluation note detailing the mobility limitation and functional impact.
- Specialty evaluation (physical/occupational therapy) when required by the specific device requested.
- Home assessment confirming accessibility.

## Exclusions

- Devices requested solely for use outside the home, or for convenience rather than medical need, are not covered.
