## Medical Necessity Criteria

Diagnostic cardiac catheterization is considered medically necessary when ONE of the following applies:

1. Patient has a positive or high-risk result on a non-invasive stress test (exercise, pharmacologic, or imaging-based).
2. Patient presents with unstable angina or a non-ST elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI).
3. Patient has new-onset heart failure with unexplained etiology where coronary artery disease must be ruled out.
4. Patient has recurrent chest pain despite optimal medical therapy with intermediate-to-high pretest probability of coronary artery disease.

## Documentation Requirements

- Stress test results (if applicable) with risk stratification.
- ECG and troponin results for acute presentations.
- Current medication regimen and symptom history.

## Exclusions

- Routine diagnostic catheterization in low-risk, asymptomatic patients without an abnormal non-invasive test is not covered.
