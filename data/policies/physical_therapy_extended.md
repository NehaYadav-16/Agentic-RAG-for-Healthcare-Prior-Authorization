## Medical Necessity Criteria

Physical therapy visits beyond the initial 12-visit allowance are considered medically necessary when ALL of the following are met:

1. Documented, measurable functional improvement from the initial visits (e.g., improved range of motion, strength grade, or validated functional score) is shown in progress notes.
2. A updated plan of care with specific, time-limited functional goals is submitted.
3. Patient has not reached a plateau in functional progress as determined by re-assessment.
4. The treating therapist documents continued skilled intervention is required (not merely a home-exercise-program-level of care).

## Documentation Requirements

- Initial evaluation and most recent re-assessment with objective measures.
- Updated plan of care signed by the referring provider or therapist.
- Visit frequency and remaining visits requested.

## Exclusions

- Maintenance therapy where the patient has plateaued and could perform a home exercise program independently is not covered beyond the initial allowance.
